## Data and derived outputs

`data/Input_USACH.xlsx` contains the consolidated municipal input used by the
analysis. `data/Output_USACH.xlsx` contains derived tables generated by the
pipeline. The source records were downloaded manually from the public SINIM
and FIGEM administrative information published by SUBDERE and transcribed or
consolidated by the authors in the workbook.

The consolidated workbook, derived output workbook, figures and methodological
document are original research artifacts produced by the authors. The MIT
license applies to the code and repository documentation; it does not alter
the public-source status or attribution requirements of the underlying
administrative records.

## Publication

The associated article is published in *Revista Políticas Públicas*:

DOI: <https://doi.org/10.35588/qezhpj36>

The 70/30 hybrid redesign mentioned in the article is a policy hypothesis, not
an implemented rule. It requires distributive simulation and institutional
review before use.
